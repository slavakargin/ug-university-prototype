# Mathematical Sciences Undergraduate Program (Prototype)

The Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Binghamton University offers courses and degree programs in mathematics, data science and statistics, and actuarial science.

## Degrees and tracks

### BA/BS in Mathematical Sciences
The BA program is designed to be flexible and allows students to tailor a course of study to their interests, with room for a second major or minor.

The BS program is more intensive and is especially appropriate for students considering graduate study or mathematically demanding careers. BS students are encouraged to consult with advisors early and plan schedules carefully.

- **Mathematics track (BA/BS)** — broad exposure across core areas of mathematics  
  → [Mathematics track (BA/BS)]({{UNIV_TRACK_MATH}})

- **Data Science & Statistics track (BA/BS)** — strong foundation in statistics and modern data tools  
  → [Data Science & Statistics track (BA/BS)]({{UNIV_TRACK_DSS}})

- **Actuarial Science track (BA/BS)** — preparation for actuarial exams and careers  
  → [Actuarial Science track (BA/BS)]({{UNIV_TRACK_ACTUARIAL}})



See the track pages above for BA/BS summaries and links to the authoritative requirements.
